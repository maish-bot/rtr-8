How a month works

One book, three chapters.

We choose the book

The pick is announced at the start of the month, with a short spoiler-free blurb so you know what you’re in for.

You read, your way

Paperback, Kindle or audiobook. Finished it, half-way, or one chapter in, it all counts here.

We meet and talk

One evening, good drinks and honest opinions. Then we vote on what to read next.

Who we are

Started by friends. Grown by word of mouth.

Read the Room began as a handful of friends meeting up to talk about the books they couldn’t stop thinking about.

It’s grown into a proper community, with new faces every month, one book at a time.

House rules
  1. No pressure to have finished it.
  2. No judgment if you didn’t.
  3. Good conversation, good company.
  4. Better recommendations than any algorithm.
  5. Every book club should feel like coming home.
